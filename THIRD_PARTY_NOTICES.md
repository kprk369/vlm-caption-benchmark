## Unsplash Dataset inputs

The study used Unsplash Lite Dataset 1.4.1. Standalone benchmark photographs,
Dataset tables, source rows, and contact sheets are not distributed as
repository files. Access to those inputs must be obtained independently under
the applicable terms.

Reference: [Unsplash Dataset Terms](https://github.com/unsplash/datasets/blob/master/TERMS.md),
particularly Sections 1.B, 3, and 7. The Dataset Terms and the general Unsplash
website license should not be treated as interchangeable permissions for
Dataset-sourced content.
